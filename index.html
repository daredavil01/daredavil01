◆ About · the whole person

I'm Sanket — a software developer from Pune who never stops moving.

By trade I'm a full-stack developer and data engineer — right now at Bridgenext, building DORA-metric pipelines and ETL flows across the GitLab API, AWS Lambda, Databricks and Tableau.

Beyond the code I'm a NAST Fellow at The Takshashila Institution, researching citizen agency in AI governance for public deployments. I care about the intersection of technology, society and public policy — not just building things, but thinking hard about what we build and why.

Outside work I run five days a week, chase Sahyadri summits on weekends, and read obsessively. I write it all down — essays, micro-posts, reflections — because keeping a record is how I stay honest with myself.
