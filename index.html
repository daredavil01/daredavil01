About · the whole person

I'm Sanket — a software developer from Pune who never stops moving. By day I'm a full-stack developer and data engineer at Bridgenext, building DORA-metric pipelines and ETL flows across the GitLab API, AWS Lambda, Databricks and Tableau.

From April 2024 to May 2025 I was a NAST Fellow at The Takshashila Institution, researching citizen agency in AI governance for public deployments — the intersection of technology, society and public policy.

I run five days a week, chase Sahyadri summits on weekends, and read obsessively. Keeping a record is how I stay honest with myself — so every number on this island comes from something I published.

18:00 · The commons · research, people, policy

Who keeps a say when the system is rolled out to them?

As a NAST Fellow at The Takshashila Institution (April 2024 – May 2025), I researched citizen agency in AI governance for public deployments — how people keep meaningful say over automated systems that are rolled out to them rather than chosen by them.

The same question runs through the websites and the data work under the banyan: foundation sites in two languages, a survey of 281 Pawra households across seven Nandurbar villages, and a sourced look at India's ethanol push. Graduate Certificate in Technology & Policy, Takshashila, 2024.
