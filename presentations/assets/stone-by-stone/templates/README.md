# Stone by Stone — doc templates

Templates for the *Stone by Stone* workshop
([deck](../../stone-by-stone.html) · [prompt pack](../prompt-pack.html)).

| File | When | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| `idea.md` | Step 1 — before any AI | You |
| `concept.md` | After Claude interviews you | Claude, from your answers |
| `technical-decisions.md` | After deep research | Claude, you approve |
| `phased-plan.md` | After deep research | Claude, you cut it down |
| `CLAUDE.md` | Phase 0, in the repo root | Claude Code, under 30 lines |

`concept.md`, `technical-decisions.md` and `phased-plan.md` go in your repo's
`docs/` folder.
